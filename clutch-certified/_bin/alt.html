Step 01 — Inspect

210 points.
No exceptions.

Every Clutch Certified vehicle is evaluated against a 210-point checklist by our certified mechanics. Structural, mechanical, electrical — everything is documented. If it doesn't pass, it doesn't move forward.

Inspection categories

210 checkpoints / 6 categories

01

Engine & Drivetrain

  • Engine oil & fluid levels
  • Coolant & hoses
  • Belts & timing
  • Transmission condition
  • Differential & axles
  • Exhaust system

02

Brakes & Suspension

  • Brake pad thickness
  • Rotor condition
  • Brake lines & fluid
  • Shocks & struts
  • Control arms & bushings
  • Wheel bearings

03

Electrical & HVAC

  • Battery & charging system
  • All lights & signals
  • Heating & A/C
  • Windows & locks
  • OBD diagnostics
  • Infotainment & cameras

04

Body & Frame

  • Panel alignment
  • Paint condition
  • Glass & seals
  • Frame integrity
  • Rust & corrosion check
  • Door & trunk operation

05

Underbody

  • Subframe & crossmembers
  • Steering rack & linkage
  • CV joints & boots
  • Fuel lines & tank
  • Exhaust mounts
  • Underbody coating

06

Road Test

  • Cold & warm start
  • Acceleration & braking
  • Steering response
  • Transmission shift quality
  • NVH evaluation
  • Up to 3 mechanic sign-offs

Up to three mechanic-led road tests. Each one signed off independently.
