Step 2 — Recondition

Reconditioned before
it's listed.

Our licensed mechanics replace worn components, perform required servicing, and address anything that falls short, bringing every vehicle up to the Clutch Certified standard.

Engine, brakes, suspension — every mechanical system is inspected and repaired to spec by our licensed technicians before the car goes anywhere near a listing.

Dents, paint chips, misaligned panels — we address cosmetic issues that affect how the car looks and holds its value. Only targeted work, done right.

Every windshield and pane of glass is checked for chips, cracks, and seal integrity. Anything below standard gets repaired or fully replaced.
