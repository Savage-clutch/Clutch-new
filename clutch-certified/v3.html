Step 2 — Recondition

Expertly serviced by
Clutch mechanics.

Anything flagged during inspection that needs to be replaced or fixed to meet our standard gets done. Oil changed, filters replaced, fluids topped, regardless of the last change. Our tire tread minimum is 4mm, double the legal requirement.

Engine, brakes, suspension — every mechanical system is inspected and repaired to spec by our licensed technicians before the car goes anywhere near a listing.

Dents, paint chips, misaligned panels — we address cosmetic issues that affect how the car looks and holds its value. Only targeted work, done right.

Every windshield and pane of glass is checked for chips, cracks, and seal integrity. Anything below standard gets repaired or fully replaced.

Tread depth is measured on all four tires against our 4mm minimum — double the legal requirement. Anything below standard gets replaced. Alignment and balance checked.

Full interior clean, exterior wash and polish, engine bay detail, and odour treatment. The car arrives to you ready — not just mechanically, but presentation-ready.
