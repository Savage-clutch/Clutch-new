The most rigorous car inspection in the industry

Every car goes through a rigorous mechanical and cosmetic inspection by our technicians. Brakes, suspension, electrical, engine, body, nothing is skipped.

Body
21 points

Every panel, gap, and surface is inspected for damage, rust, prior repairs, and paint consistency. We check structural integrity and cosmetic condition to ensure nothing is hidden before listing.

Road Test
79 points

Three separate mechanic-led road tests validate real-world performance. We assess acceleration, braking, handling, noise, and vibration — the kind of issues that only reveal themselves when the car is actually moving.

Engine
41 points

A full engine inspection covers fluid levels, belts, hoses, sensors, and emissions. We run diagnostic scans to catch any stored fault codes and verify the engine performs within manufacturer specifications.

Suspension
16 points

Shocks, struts, control arms, bushings, and wheel bearings are all assessed for wear. Suspension condition directly affects ride quality and safety — we don't pass anything that compromises either.

Steering
8 points

Rack and pinion, power steering fluid, tie rods, and alignment are checked. Any play or drift in the steering system is flagged and resolved before the car reaches you.

Lights
10 points

Every exterior and interior light is tested — headlights, indicators, brake lights, reverse lights, and cabin lighting. We also verify all sensors tied to the lighting system are functioning correctly.

Underbody
11 points

The undercarriage is inspected for frame damage, rust, exhaust condition, and fluid leaks. An underbody inspection often reveals the car's true history in ways a visual exterior check cannot.

Tires & Brakes
21 points

Tread depth is measured on all four tires. Brake pad thickness, rotor condition, and caliper function are all checked. Anything below safe operating standards is replaced before the car is listed.
