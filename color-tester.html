Rule 01
Set headlines in one colour only.
Use White or Clutch Red on dark backgrounds. Use Plum on light backgrounds. Maximum contrast, zero guesswork.
Rule 02
Highlight with a single accent.
Change just one key phrase to a second brand colour. Plum on Red, Red on Lavender. Guides the eye without turning the layout into confetti.
Rule 03
Lock the logo in its strongest form.
White logo on dark backgrounds. Red logo on light backgrounds. Never place the logo on a colour that reduces its contrast.
